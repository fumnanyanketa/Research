# Technology Literacy — Foundations Curriculum (draft)

The on-ramp track. Builds everyday digital confidence so AI (and modern life in Finland) becomes usable. Five core monthly modules plus a bridge into AI. Hands-on, laptops/phones in hand.

**Format per session:** ~3 hours · demonstrate → "you try it" → short recap · everyone leaves with a one-page cheat-sheet and one thing they can now do.

> Draft for editing. Re-order or merge modules freely once you see your pilot group's level.

---

## Module 1 — Your device, without fear
**Goal:** be comfortable and in control of your phone and laptop.
- Phone & laptop basics: settings, updates, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, accounts.
- **Storage & "my phone is always full"**: what fills it (photos, WhatsApp auto-downloads), how to clear it, turning off auto-download.
- Battery, notifications, "do not disturb," accessibility (text size, etc.).
- Keyboard & gesture shortcuts that save time.

**Hands-on:** free up storage on your own phone; turn off WhatsApp auto-downloads; tidy the home screen.
**Leave with:** a phone that isn't full, and the settings map.

## Module 2 — Files & the cloud (Google Drive)
**Goal:** never lose a document again; be ready to use the shared workspace.
- What "the cloud" means; files vs apps; where things actually live.
- **Google Drive**: creating, uploading, organizing into folders, search.
- **Google Docs & Sheets**: create, edit, format, download/export.
- **Sharing**: links, view vs edit access, commenting; our shared workspace.

**Hands-on:** create a folder, make a Doc, share it with the person next to you, leave a comment.
**Leave with:** access to the community shared Drive and the confidence to use it.
*(This module directly enables the team's idea database + workspace.)*

## Module 3 — The internet & smart searching
**Goal:** find anything, safely, and keep it organized.
- Browsers, tabs, bookmarks, history; multiple accounts/profiles.
- **Searching well**: keywords, questions, narrowing results, evaluating sources.
- Downloads, PDFs, filling forms online.
- Maps, translation, reviews — practical daily tools.

**Hands-on:** research a real task (e.g. find an official service, compare two options), bookmark the result.
**Leave with:** a search-like-a-pro cheat-sheet.

## Module 4 — Communicating & staying organized
**Goal:** email, calendars, and calls that make you look professional.
- **Email** that gets replies: structure, subject lines, attachments, etiquette (vs WhatsApp).
- **Calendars**: events, reminders, invites, sharing availability.
- **Video calls** (Meet/Zoom/Teams): joining, camera/mic, screen-share, basic etiquette.
- WhatsApp for groups: keeping it clean, media settings, voice-note etiquette.

**Hands-on:** send a well-structured email; put a real event in your calendar with a reminder; do a 2-minute test video call.
**Leave with:** a reusable email template and a working calendar.

## Module 5 — Staying safe online
**Goal:** protect yourself, your money, and your data.
- **Passwords**: strong + unique, a password manager, why reuse is dangerous.
- **Two-factor authentication (2FA)** and how to set it up.
- **Scams & phishing**: how to spot them (fake messages, "your account is locked," money requests), what to do.
- **Privacy**: app permissions, what you share, location, public Wi-Fi basics.
- Backups (so a lost phone isn't a disaster).

**Hands-on:** turn on 2FA for one important account; set up a password manager; spot the scam in 5 example messages.
**Leave with:** a personal security checklist.

## Module 6 — Digital life in Finland (practical admin)
**Goal:** navigate the digital services daily life here depends on.
- Online banking & strong authentication (the Finnish bank-ID world), staying safe.
- Booking appointments (health, services), official portals, reading official messages.
- Digital documents, e-signatures, scanning with your phone.
- Where to get help when stuck (libraries, official help, the community).

**Hands-on:** practice a real task safely (e.g. book/locate an appointment, scan a document to PDF).
**Leave with:** confidence to handle digital admin alone.

## Module 7 (bridge) — Make things look good + meet AI
**Goal:** light a spark for the AI track.
- Quick wins with **Canva** (a poster, a simple CV, a social post) — visible, motivating results.
- "**What is AI, really?**" in plain language — it's already in your phone; it's a tool, not magic.
- First friendly chat with an AI assistant (a question, a translation, a rewrite).

**Hands-on:** make one nice-looking thing in Canva; ask an AI assistant one useful question.
**Leave with:** excitement + a path into the AI Literacy track.

---

## Facilitation notes
- **Demonstrate, then they do it** — no slides-only sessions. Walk around and help.
- **Use their real problems** as the examples (a full phone, a job application, a scam text they got).
- **Pair people up** — confident learners help newer ones; builds the community feel.
- **Plain language** — translate every technical word the moment you use it.
- **Pace for the slowest, offer "extra" for the fastest** so nobody is bored or lost.

## Materials per module
- One-page cheat-sheet (kept by participants), a short slide deck, and 3–5 real practice tasks.
- A spare laptop/phone for those without one.
- Everything stored in the shared Drive **Programs** folder.

## After this track
A graduate can: manage their device and storage, use Google Drive/Docs, search and stay safe online, handle email/calendar/calls, navigate Finnish digital services, and is ready for the **AI Literacy** track.
