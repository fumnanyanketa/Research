# Technology & AI Literacy Program — Overview

The community upskilling program agreed at the 20 June kickoff. It was renamed from "AI literacy" to **technology literacy** because people need basic digital confidence before AI is useful ("even to use AI, you have to know how to use the tools, your phone, your laptop, Google Drive").

So the program is **two tracks that chain together**:

1. **Foundations (Technology Literacy)** — digital confidence: devices, files & the cloud, internet, communication, online safety, digital life in Finland.
2. **AI Literacy** — using AI well: what it is, prompting, AI for daily life/work/business, automations, building, agents, and responsible use.

## Who it's for
Immigrants and members of the African (and wider) community in Finland, with **mixed digital literacy** — from "my phone is always full and I don't know why" to "I want to build my own app." People join at the right level.

## Goals
- Build everyday **digital confidence** so no one is left behind as everything becomes AI-powered.
- Move people from **consumers to builders** — able to use AI for life, work, job-hunting, and their own hustle.
- Feed the wider mission: a warm pipeline for **Africa Week 2027's tech day**, demo projects to showcase, and skills that help people earn.

## Format (from the meetings)
- **Monthly**, ~**3 hours**, **hands-on** (not lectures) — demonstrate, then "you try it."
- Participants **bring their own laptop/phone**; the office space is the venue.
- **Small cohort first** (a pilot, ~8–12 people) to calibrate, then grow.
- **Facilitation style**, patient and practical; jargon translated to plain language.
- Consider a **women-only variant** for sessions where some women won't attend mixed groups.
- A **closed-cohort** rhythm (like the Girls Club): first 1–2 sessions open, then a committed group, so people progress together.

## The 12-month arc (start Aug/Sep 2026)
| Months | Track | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1–5 | Foundations | Devices → files/cloud → internet → communication → online safety & digital life in Finland |
| 6 | Bridge | "What is AI and why should I care?" |
| 7–12 | AI Literacy | Prompting → AI for life/work → AI for business → automations → building → agents → responsible use → demo day |

People who are already comfortable with the basics can join from the AI Literacy track; absolute beginners start at Foundations.

## Pathways out (why people stay)
- **Jobs**: stronger CVs/applications, digital admin confidence, employability.
- **Their hustle**: content, social media, customer comms, simple tools — ties to the AI Nativity agency idea.
- **Africa Week 2027**: alumni become the volunteer/facilitator bench and exhibit demo projects.
- **Certificates** of completion (credibility, and useful for Business Helsinki / job applications).

## Funding & partners
- **City digital-skills training programs** (one was already found — funds "courses strengthening digital skills").
- **Anthropic** nonprofit program + possible API credits / a guest session (send the official email).
- Platform/tool sponsors; library and university (Metropolia-style) partnerships.

## How we'll measure it
- Attendance + retention per cohort.
- A simple **before/after confidence self-rating** (1–5 on key skills).
- Projects produced (count + showcased at Africa Week).
- Post-session feedback (one question: "what's one thing you can now do that you couldn't before?").

## Materials & logistics
- One **shared slide/handout per session** + a one-page **cheat-sheet** people keep.
- Everything lives in the **Programs** folder of the shared Drive workspace.
- A **WhatsApp subgroup** for the cohort (reminders, links, Q&A between sessions).
- A spare laptop or two for people who don't have one.
