# AI Literacy Curriculum (draft)

The second track: using AI well and confidently — for daily life, work, job-hunting, your hustle, and eventually building. Follows the Technology Literacy foundations. Hands-on; everyone works on a real AI tool each session.

**Format per session:** ~3 hours · demonstrate → "you try it" on your own task → share → recap · leave with a cheat-sheet and something you actually made.

> Draft for editing. Tools move fast — keep the *skills* fixed and swap the specific tools as needed closer to each session.

---

## Module 1 — What AI actually is (demystify)
**Goal:** replace fear/hype with a clear, simple mental model.
- AI is a tool, not magic; it's already in your phone, keyboard, maps.
- Plain-language ideas: what an "LLM" / chatbot is, what an "agent" is, what AI can and can't do.
- The landscape: assistants (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini), free vs paid, privacy basics.
- Why this matters now: "one way or another, everything is becoming AI-powered."

**Hands-on:** set up a free AI assistant account; ask it three real questions.
**Leave with:** an account, and the confidence that this is learnable.

## Module 2 — Prompting fundamentals
**Goal:** get genuinely useful answers, not vague ones.
- How to talk to AI: give it a role, context, the goal, and the format you want.
- Iterating: refining, asking for changes, "make it shorter/simpler/in my voice."
- Useful patterns: summarize, explain like I'm new, draft, rewrite, compare, brainstorm.
- Checking the answer (AI can be confidently wrong — verify what matters).

**Hands-on:** take one real task and improve the result through 3 rounds of prompting.
**Leave with:** a personal prompt cheat-sheet.

## Module 3 — AI for everyday life
**Goal:** save time on real, daily things.
- Writing & replying (messages, letters, official responses), in a clearer voice.
- **Translating & language learning** — huge for this community.
- Summarizing long things (documents, articles, voice notes).
- Planning, explaining, learning a new topic step by step.

**Hands-on:** each person solves one real-life task (translate a letter, summarize a document, draft a reply).
**Leave with:** 3 things AI now does for them weekly.

## Module 4 — AI for work & job-hunting
**Goal:** become more employable and effective at work.
- **CVs & cover letters** tailored to a job posting; preparing for interviews.
- Understanding job ads, writing applications, professional emails.
- Everyday work tasks: notes, summaries, spreadsheets, presentations.
- Using AI at work responsibly (what not to paste in).

**Hands-on:** tailor your own CV to a real job posting with AI.
**Leave with:** an improved CV and an application workflow.

## Module 5 — AI for your hustle / business
**Goal:** use AI to get and serve customers (ties to the AI Nativity agency).
- **Content & social media**: posts, captions, short scripts, a week of content in an hour.
- Customer communication: replies, FAQs, simple booking messages.
- Branding basics with AI + Canva: logo ideas, flyers, simple brand kit.
- Bookkeeping/admin helpers; lead generation thinking ("more clients → more money").

**Hands-on:** create a week of social content for a real (or imagined) small business.
**Leave with:** a content batch + a reusable content prompt.

## Module 6 — Automations (no-code)
**Goal:** make repetitive tasks happen by themselves.
- What "automation" means; spotting tasks worth automating.
- Connecting tools (forms → sheets → notifications) with no-code tools.
- A simple end-to-end workflow they can reuse.

**Hands-on:** build one small automation (e.g. a form that collects responses into a sheet and pings you).
**Leave with:** one working automation.

## Module 7 — Building with AI (intro)
**Goal:** the "consumer → builder" leap; make a real thing.
- Code is cheap now: building a simple page/app by describing it.
- Tools: AI assistants for building (Claude), and where things get hosted (the kind of stack behind our own community site).
- The real work is **fine-tuning from feedback**, not the first draft.
- A taste of what's possible, without needing to be an engineer.

**Hands-on:** build and publish a one-page site (e.g. a personal or business landing page).
**Leave with:** a live link they made themselves.

## Module 8 — Agents & what's next
**Goal:** understand the next wave in plain terms.
- What an "agent" is (an AI that uses tools to do tasks), with simple examples (a salon's booking helper, a lead-gen helper).
- Where agents help a small business; where they don't.
- Keeping up: how to keep learning as tools change.

**Hands-on:** map one real task that an agent could do for a business you know.
**Leave with:** an agent use-case for their own context.

## Module 9 — Responsible & safe AI
**Goal:** use AI ethically and protect yourself.
- **Privacy & data**: what's safe to share with AI, what isn't.
- **Hallucinations & verification**: trust but check.
- **Bias & fairness**; AI's limits.
- **Copyright & ownership** (the real debate: who owns AI-assisted work — relevant to music/art creators in the community).
- Scams that use AI (deepfakes, voice cloning) and how to stay safe.

**Hands-on:** spot the problems in a set of example AI uses; write your own "responsible use" rules.
**Leave with:** a personal AI-safety checklist.

## Module 10 — Demo Day (capstone)
**Goal:** celebrate, consolidate, and feed Africa Week.
- Everyone shows one thing they built or now do with AI.
- Certificates of completion.
- Selecting projects to **showcase at Africa Week 2027**.
- Inviting standouts onto the volunteer/facilitator bench.

**Hands-on:** present your project (2 minutes each).
**Leave with:** a certificate, a portfolio piece, and a next step.

## Facilitation notes
- **Everyone works on their own real task** each session — relevance drives retention.
- **Demonstrate live, then circulate** and help; pair confident learners with newer ones.
- **Translate jargon instantly**; "an agent is just an AI that can do tasks for you, like a smart assistant."
- **Tools will change** — teach the skill (prompting, verifying, building), not just the button.
- Keep a **women-only variant** available where it widens access.

## Materials per module
- Cheat-sheet + short deck + 3–5 real tasks + example prompts.
- Shared Drive **Programs** folder for all materials; a cohort WhatsApp group for links/reminders.
