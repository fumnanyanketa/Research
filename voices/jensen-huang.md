# Jensen Huang

> Co-founder and CEO of NVIDIA, the dominant supplier of AI compute, currently evangelizing agentic AI and fighting US export controls on China.

Window: 2026-04-11 → 2026-06-11 · Compiled: 2026-06-11

## Interviews & podcast appearances

### 2026-04-15 — Dwarkesh Podcast: "TPU competition, why we should sell chips to China, & Nvidia's supply chain moat"
- Link: https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/jensen-huang (analysis: https://thezvi.substack.com/p/on-dwarkesh-patels-podcast-with-nvidia, https://www.techloy.com/jensen-huang-on-dwarkesh-patel-podcast-8-revelations-on-anthropic-china-and-nvidias-roadmap/)
- Key ideas:
  - Framed the AI industry as five layers — energy, chips, infrastructure, models, applications — and opposed policy that "sacrifices one layer to benefit another."
  - Argued for continued AI chip exports to China ("increasing sales of American technology is beneficial"), debating Patel on national-security objections without directly answering the most sensitive ones (per https://www.visiontimes.com/2026/04/20/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-faces-heated-debate-over-ai-chip-exports-to-china-amid-security-concerns.html).
  - Said Nvidia once had ~90% of China's AI compute market and has effectively lost access due to export restrictions.
  - On Anthropic running mostly on TPUs/Trainium: Nvidia wasn't in a position early on to write the multi-billion-dollar founding equity checks Google and AWS did.
  - Acknowledged "always some irrationality somewhere in the stock market" but rejected the big-AI-bubble thesis, pointing to structural change (https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/ai-video/2026/jensen-huang-on-the-modest-ai-bubble-and-the-flawed-decoupling-of-us-and-china-tech/).

### 2026-05-04 — Milken Institute Global Conference: conversation with Becky Quick
- Link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dUFis1dte14 (coverage: https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/04/as-workers-worry-about-ai-nvidias-jensen-huang-says-ai-is-creating-an-enormous-number-of-jobs/)
- Key ideas:
  - Pushed back on AI-job-loss anxiety: AI is "creating an enormous number of jobs" — an industrial-scale generator of employment across construction, power, and trades, not just software.

### ~2026-05-11 — Carnegie Mellon University commencement address
- Link: https://fortune.com/2026/05/11/jensen-huang-tells-electricians-and-plumbers-this-is-your-time/ (see also https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/your-career-starts-at-the-beginning-of-the-ai-revolution-nvidia-ceo-tells-graduates/)
- Key ideas:
  - "AI gives America the opportunity to build again. Electricians, plumbers, iron workers, technicians, builders — this is your time."
  - Told graduates their careers start "at the beginning of the AI revolution"; the AI jobs market extends far beyond software and engineering degrees.

### 2026-05-20/21 — Nvidia Q1 FY2027 earnings call + CNBC interview with Sara Eisen
- Link: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/nvidia-nvda-earnings-report-q1-2027.html and https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/05/21/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-in-ai-compute-is-revenue.html (see also https://intellectia.ai/blog/nvda-stock-earnings-analysis-may-2026, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/21/nvidia-jensen-huang-china-ai-chip-market-huawei.html)
- Key ideas:
  - Record quarter: $81.6B revenue (+85% YoY) on the call where he said "Blackwell sales are off the charts, and cloud GPUs are sold out."
  - "In AI, compute is revenue" — every deployed token of compute maps to customer revenue; cited an order backlog of at least $1 trillion, double a year earlier.
  - Expects Nvidia to "be constrained throughout the entire life of Vera Rubin."
  - Said Nvidia has "largely conceded" China's AI chip market to Huawei because of US export controls; discussed the Anthropic partnership.

### 2026-06-01 — GTC Taipei at COMPUTEX 2026 keynote
- Link: https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-gtc-taipei-computex-2026-news/ (transcript/takeaways: https://semiconalpha.substack.com/p/nvidia-keynote-computex-2026-key, https://www.crnasia.com/news/2026/components-and-peripherals/three-key-takeaways-from-nvidia-at-computex-2026, replay: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wSp6AiNIrsY)
- Key ideas:

  - "AI agents will be the largest users of computing"; the Vera CPU is "the first CPU designed for that future," claiming 1.8x faster task completion vs x86.
  - Announced RTX Spark for Windows PCs (CUDA + RTX + AI in one superchip), in laptops/desktops this fall from ASUS, Dell, HP, Lenovo, Microsoft Surface, MSI.

### 2026-06-04 / 2026-06-08 — Declines Senator Warren's invitation to testify on China chip sales
- Link: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/04/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-warren-senate-hearing-china-ai-chips.html and https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/08/nvidia-jensen-huang-senate-elizabeth-warren-ai-china-export-controls.html (also https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-declines-warren-senate-testify-ai-hearing-rcna349034)
- Key claims:
  - Warren invited Huang to a Senate hearing on AI chip sales to China and national security; Huang declined to testify (June 8), consistent with his position that export controls have handed the China market to Huawei.

## Core theses right now

- Agentic AI is the new industrial workload: agents will be the largest users of computing, every token is a revenue unit, and "compute is revenue."
- Demand structurally exceeds supply — Blackwell "off the charts," cloud GPUs sold out, a $1T+ backlog, and constraint expected through the entire Vera Rubin generation.
- US export controls are self-defeating: Nvidia went from ~90% of China's AI compute market to "largely conceding" it to Huawei; selling American technology abroad strengthens, not weakens, the US stack.
- AI is a net job creator at industrial scale — especially for the trades (electricians, plumbers, builders) needed to construct AI factories; students shouldn't fear studying anything.
- The AI boom has pockets of market irrationality but is not a bubble; the underlying change is structural (full-stack: energy, chips, infrastructure, models, applications).
