# Mark Zuckerberg

> Founder, Chairman and CEO of Meta Platforms, currently driving the company's pivot to "personal superintelligence" via Meta Superintelligence Labs.

Window: 2026-04-11 → 2026-06-11 · Compiled: 2026-06-11

## Interviews & podcast appearances

### 2026-04-29 — Meta Q1 2026 earnings call (webcast, with analyst Q&A)
- Link: https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/04/29/meta-meta-q1-2026-earnings-call-transcript/ (see also https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/29/meta-q1-earnings-report-2026.html)
- Key ideas:
  - Called Q1 a "milestone quarter," citing the release of Muse Spark, the first model from Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL), and said Meta is "on track to deliver personal superintelligence to billions of people."
  - On self-improving AI: "companies that don't do that are not going to be leading labs; they're not going to produce leading products."
  - Said the superintelligence team is building AI agents on top of Muse Spark: "Our goal is not just to deliver Meta AI as an assistant, but to deliver agents that can understand your goals and then work day and night to help you achieve them" (as reported by Engadget: https://www.engadget.com/2160792/mark-zuckerberg-says-meta-is-working-on-ai-agents-for-personal-and-business-use/).
  - Claimed AI productivity gains: "We are seeing more and more examples where one or two people are building something in a week that would have previously taken dozens of people months."
  - Raised 2026 capex guidance to $125–145 billion, citing component pricing and data center costs; framed AI as the core driver of ad performance gains across Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp.

### 2026-05-27 — CNBC: Meta cloud computing business "definitely on the table"
- Link: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/27/mark-zuckerberg-says-meta-starting-cloud-business-on-the-table.html
- Key ideas:
  - Said a Meta cloud/compute business is "definitely on the table"; companies frequently approach Meta asking about API services or compute resources.
  - Noted Meta is the only one of the four giant U.S. hyperscalers without a cloud infrastructure and services business.

## Core theses right now

- Meta's mission is "personal superintelligence" for billions: every person gets an AI agent that understands their goals and "works day and night" on their behalf, embedded across Meta's apps and AI glasses.
- Self-improving AI is the gating capability — labs that don't build it won't stay frontier labs or ship leading products.
- The AI buildout justifies enormous spend ($125–145B 2026 capex) and headcount restructuring; AI is already paying for itself via ad performance.
- Meta is moving from open-source (Llama) to closed frontier models (Muse Spark) and toward monetizing AI directly — paid subscriptions, premium high-compute agents, and possibly a Meta cloud business.
- AI will dramatically change work this year; small teams with agents replace large teams, but people become "more important, not less."
