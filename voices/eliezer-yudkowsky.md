# Eliezer Yudkowsky

> Decision theorist, co-founder of the Machine Intelligence Research Institute (MIRI), co-author of "If Anyone Builds It, Everyone Dies," and the most prominent advocate of halting the race to superintelligent AI.

Window: 2026-04-11 → 2026-06-11 · Compiled: 2026-06-11

## Interviews & podcast appearances

### 2026-05-05 — YouTube (Liron Shapira's channel / Doom Debates): $10,000 debate vs. anonymous AI lab director
- Link: https://lironshapira.substack.com/p/eliezer-yudkowsky-debates-ai-lab-director
- Key ideas:
  - An anonymous donor ("@47fucb4r8c69323") paid $10,000 for Yudkowsky to publicly debate a "secret AI lab director."
  - Yudkowsky maintained that the probability of extinction from superintelligent AI is too high not to speak out, defending his "if anyone builds it, everyone dies" framing against the charge that it could incite unstable individuals against AI researchers.
  - He called on governments to immediately pursue international treaties limiting proliferation of advanced AI hardware/compute and training scale, rather than relying on labs' internal safety protocols (per https://www.winzheng.com/en/article/eliezer-yudkowsky-10000-dollar-debate-anonymous-ai-lab-director-will-ai-kill-us-all-2026).
  - On interpretability: we have not extracted the algorithms that explain LLMs' qualitative intelligence, so claims that "we understand how they work" are false; his opponent dismissed this as misunderstanding text modeling.

## Blog posts, essays & notable statements

### 2026-04-13 — "Only Law Can Prevent Extinction"
- Link: https://askwhocastsai.substack.com/p/only-law-can-prevent-extinction-by (AI-narrated republication of Yudkowsky's essay, posted 2026-04-13)
- Key claims:
  - The existential risk from artificial superintelligence cannot be mitigated by individual action, corporate self-regulation, or localized prohibitions.
  - Only coordinated international law — binding, global, enforceable — can prevent extinction.

### 2026-05-04 — "Irretrievability; or, Murphy's Curse of Oneshotness upon ASI" (LessWrong)
- Link: https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/fbrz9xhKpEeTKw5zL/irretrievability-or-murphy-s-curse-of-oneshotness-upon-asi
- Key claims:
  - Builds on his 2022 "List of Lethalities": ASI development is a one-shot problem — you do not get to retry after a failed attempt at aligning superintelligence.
  - "Irretrievability" frames why iterative, empirical safety approaches that work elsewhere in engineering fail for ASI.

## Core theses right now

- Building superintelligent AI under anything like current conditions means everyone dies; the extinction probability is high enough to justify maximal public alarm.
- ASI is a one-shot, irretrievable problem — humanity cannot iterate its way to alignment, because the first failed attempt forecloses any retry.
- Lab self-regulation and internal safety protocols are inadequate; only binding international law and treaties limiting advanced compute and training scale can prevent extinction.
- We do not actually understand how LLMs work — no extracted algorithms explain their intelligence — so confidence in controllability is unjustified.
- He remains willing to debate critics directly (including anonymous lab insiders) and rejects the argument that extinction rhetoric should be toned down for fear of inciting violence.
