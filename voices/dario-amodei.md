## Blog posts, essays & notable statements

### 2026-06-10 — "Policy on the AI Exponential" (essay, darioamodei.com)
- Link: https://www.axios.com/2026/06/10/anthropic-ceo-government-block-dangerous-ai
- Key claims:
  - Published one day after Anthropic released Claude Fable 5 (June 9), its first publicly available Mythos-class model (https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/09/anthropic-mythos-claude-fable-5.html).
  - The US government should hold legal authority to "block or deter" deployment of frontier AI models that "pose a significant risk of catastrophic harms."
  - Proposed a "compute threshold": any model trained above it automatically triggers an independent capabilities investigation before public launch.
  - Called for FAA-style rigor — technical testing and auditing of models like airplanes, with no release if safety standards aren't met (https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropic-ceo-calls-for-faa-style-regulation-of-powerful-ai-models-what-enterprises-should-know).
  - His framing: "AI is progressing extremely fast—much faster than the policy process was built to handle. The essay lays out where I think the technology is now, and the action needed to close the gap." (https://beincrypto.com/dario-amodei-ai-policy-essay/)
  - On jobs: says he warns of displacement not as a "prophet of doom" but so "policymakers and the private sector ... have the best chance to adapt and respond"; proposed better displacement-tracking data, pro-employment policy incentives, and "mechanisms such as universal basic income" funded by taxes on "relevant companies" or higher capital gains taxes (https://fortune.com/2026/06/11/anthropic-dario-amodei-ai-jobs-tax-displacement-fund/).
  - Accompanied by ~$350M in Anthropic commitments: a $200M Economic Futures Research Fund and a $150M national fellowship program for early-career Americans (https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318217/20260611/ai-regulation-push-amodei-demands-power-blocking-unsafe-models-anthropic-pledges-350-million.htm).

### 2026-05-26 — Reported softening of his jobs-apocalypse warnings
- Link: https://fortune.com/2026/05/26/sam-altman-dario-amodei-walking-back-ai-jobs-apocalypse-prophecies-ipo/

His 2026 Dwarkesh Podcast appearance was recorded February 13, 2026, also pre-window (https://thezvi.substack.com/p/on-dwarkesh-patels-2026-podcast-with).

## Core theses right now

- AI capability is on an exponential that is outpacing the policy process; governments need legal authority now to test, audit, and block dangerous frontier models (FAA-style regulation plus a compute threshold).
- Labor-market disruption from AI is real and imminent enough to pre-fund: track displacement data, incentivize employment, and prepare UBI-like mechanisms financed partly by taxing AI companies themselves — though his publicly stated 50%-of-entry-level-jobs warnings have softened toward "automation may expand work."
- Safety leadership and commercial leadership are compatible: Anthropic shipped its most powerful public model (Claude Fable 5) the day before demanding stricter rules for everyone, with vetted-user restrictions on its full cyber capabilities (https://www.computing.co.uk/news/2026/ai/anthropic-unveils-claude-fable-5-but-reserves-full-cyber-capabilities-for-vetted-users).
- Culture is the durable moat — he devotes 40-50% of his time to it and has delegated nearly all operations to Daniela Amodei so he can focus on strategy, research direction, and essays.
- The private sector should self-bind: Anthropic is voluntarily putting $350M toward studying and cushioning AI's economic fallout, effectively proposing to tax itself.
