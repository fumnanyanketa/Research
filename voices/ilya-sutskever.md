## Interviews & podcast appearances

### ~Mid-May 2026 (date unconfirmed) — Testimony, Musk v. OpenAI trial (not an interview, but his only public statements in the window)
- Link: https://thenextweb.com/news/ilya-sutskever-7bn-openai-stake-disclosure (see also https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/sy00kqxljgg and https://fortune.com/2026/05/12/sam-altman-testimony-open-ai-elon-musk-trial/)
- Key ideas:
  - Disclosed that his ownership stake in OpenAI is worth approximately $7 billion, making him one of the largest individual OpenAI shareholders.
  - Confirmed he wrote a 2023 memo to OpenAI's board describing Altman as pitting executives against one another and exhibiting a "consistent pattern of lying."
  - Said he later signed the letter supporting Altman's reinstatement to keep the company from being destroyed, and that he regretted his role in the ouster attempt.
  - On Musk: admired him early as an entrepreneur, but Musk's push for a controlling stake in OpenAI "just felt aggressive to me."
  - Euronews (2026-05-21) confirmed the stake disclosure happened "last week," placing testimony around mid-May 2026: https://www.euronews.com/business/2026/05/21/ai-bidding-wars-the-talent-making-a-fortune-as-big-tech-firms-fight-it-out

## Blog posts, essays & notable statements

None authored by Sutskever in the window. Relevant third-party reporting:

### 2026-06-06 — StartupHub.ai analysis: "Ilya Sutskever's SSI: The $32B Bet on a Post-Scaling Future"
- Link: https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/ai-figures/2026/figure-ilya-sutskever-ssi-financial-breakdown-2026-06-06
- Key claims (about, not by, Sutskever):
  - SSI has raised ~$6 billion total at a $32 billion valuation since its June 2024 founding, remains ~27 employees (as of 2026-05-26), and still has no commercial product — by design, until the safe-superintelligence mission is complete.
  - SSI declined a Meta acquisition approach; Sutskever became CEO after co-founder Daniel Gross left for Meta in June 2025.

### Background (pre-window, still the best statement of his current views) — Dwarkesh Podcast, Nov 2025: "We're moving from the age of scaling to the age of research"
- Link: https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/ilya-sutskever-2 (summaries: https://the-decoder.com/ilya-sutskever-says-a-new-learning-paradigm-is-necessary-and-is-already-chasing-it/, https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/h1fudk7z11x)
- Key claims:
  - 2012–2020 was the age of research, 2020–2025 the age of scaling; returns from scaling are diminishing and the field is returning to an age of research where ideas, not compute, are the bottleneck.
  - AGI will be a continual learner that "learns on the job," not a static pre-trained system; models today show "jaggedness" — strong on benchmarks, weak in the real world.
  - Timeline: 5–20 years to a system that learns as efficiently as a human; even in a "straight shot" scenario he would do a gradual release.
  - Alignment target: AI that cares about "sentient life."

## Core theses right now

- (Carried from late 2025; nothing in the window contradicts it) Scaling is hitting diminishing returns; the bottleneck is ideas, and SSI is betting on new learning paradigms — continual, human-like learning — rather than bigger pre-training runs.
- SSI stays product-less and quiet on purpose: one mission, safe superintelligence, insulated from commercial pressure ($6B raised, $32B valuation, ~27 staff, acquisition approaches declined).
- Superintelligence should be released gradually and aligned to care about sentient life.
- His public profile in this window is almost entirely legal, not technical: the Musk v. OpenAI trial revealed his ~$7B OpenAI stake and his 2023 doubts about Altman's honesty.
