## Core theses right now

(Inferred from fund positioning and reporting in the window, since he has made no new public statements.)

- AGI-scale AI remains the defining economic and geopolitical event of the decade; his 2024 "Situational Awareness" thesis still anchors everything he does.
- The binding constraint on AI is physical — power, data centers, and chips — so the fund is long energy, AI cloud infrastructure (e.g., Nebius), and miners-turned-AI-hosts rather than chatbot/app makers.
- He sees froth in AI chip equities specifically: billions in put options against major semiconductor names while staying long the buildout.
- Frontier lab equity (a ~20% Anthropic position) is a core expression of the superintelligence thesis.
- He continues to influence the AI discourse through capital allocation rather than public commentary; expect essays/interviews to remain rare.
