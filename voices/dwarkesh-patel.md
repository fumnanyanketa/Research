### 2026-05-08 — David Reich (Harvard geneticist), with collaborator Ali Akbari: "Why the Bronze Age was an inflection point in human evolution"
- Link: https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/david-reich-2
- Topics: new paper overturning the consensus that natural selection went dormant after the agricultural revolution — selection actually sped up; genetic predictor of cognitive performance rose ~1 SD over 10,000 years, mostly 4,000–2,000 years ago; selection "went bonkers" in the Bronze Age across immune function, body fat, and cognition ( https://x.com/dwarkesh_sp/status/2052798237828960334 ).

### 2026-05-15 — Eric Jang (ex-VP of AI, 1X; ex-Google DeepMind Robotics): "Building AlphaGo from scratch"
- Link: https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/eric-jang
- Topics: rebuilding AlphaGo for under $10k on sabbatical; AlphaGo as the cleanest worked example of the primitives of intelligence (search, learning from experience, self-play); how MCTS sidesteps the credit-assignment problem that plagues naive policy-gradient RL in LLMs.

### 2026-05-22 — Reiner Pope (CEO, MatX): "Chip design from the bottom up"
- Link: https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/reiner-pope-2
- Topics: second blackboard lecture — from logic gates up to why GPUs, TPUs, FPGAs, and the human brain each look the way they do; systolic arrays, pipeline registers, cache vs scratchpad, CPU vs GPU core design, brains vs chips.

### 2026-06-04 — Alex Imas (Director of AGI Economics, Google DeepMind) & Phil Trammell (Head of Economics, Epoch AI): "What remains scarce after AGI?"
- Link: https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/alex-imas-phil-trammell
- Topics: what economics predicts for wages and the labor share under advanced automation; how to tax and redistribute AGI-generated wealth; what stays scarce — including "relational" services where replacing a human with an AI lowers willingness-to-pay (trust, empathy, authenticity).

## Core theses right now

- AI timelines are real but somewhat longer than many of his guests claim: continual learning is a huge bottleneck and extended computer use may take years to sort out ( https://x.com/dwarkesh_sp/status/1929598755508310090 ).
- The economics of AGI deserve rigorous treatment now — what stays scarce, what happens to wages, and how AGI wealth gets redistributed (Imas/Trammell episode, blog prize).
- Hardware and infrastructure are the binding constraints of the AI buildout — hence repeated deep dives on chips, supply chains, and training/serving math (Huang, Pope x2).
- The show is shifting toward pedagogy and primary research: blackboard lectures, replication projects (Jang's AlphaGo rebuild), and funding outside thinkers via the blog prize.
- Intelligence itself is an empirical subject spanning biology and machines — from Bronze Age selection on cognition (Reich) to the primitives of search and self-play (Jang).
