# Alexandr Wang

> Meta's Chief AI Officer and head of Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL), the Scale AI founder hired in June 2025 in a ~$14B deal.

Window: 2026-04-11 → 2026-06-11 · Compiled: 2026-06-11

## Interviews & podcast appearances

### 2026-05-13 — Core Memory podcast (EP 71): "Meta's AI Chief On AI Beef, New Models And Life With Zuck"
- Link: https://www.corememory.com/p/metas-ai-chief-alex-wang-muse-spark-ai-wars
- Key ideas:
  - His first public podcast appearance in months; discussed the Muse Spark release and MSL's nine-month ground-up rebuild of Meta's AI stack (infrastructure, architecture, data pipelines).
  - Laid out the "personal superintelligence" goal — an agent that thinks, plans and acts on each user's behalf.
  - Covered Meta's competitive position in the "AI wars" and working with Zuckerberg; episode also featured MSL leaders Nat Friedman, Daniel Gross and Shengjia Zhao.

### 2026-06-05 — Bloomberg Tech Summit, San Francisco (on-stage interview)
- Link: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-05/meta-ai-chief-sees-opportunity-in-models-giving-health-advice (see also https://letsdatascience.com/news/alexandr-wang-calls-muse-spark-an-appetizer-0f0c1905 and https://cryptobriefing.com/meta-ai-wang-health-capabilities/)
- Key ideas:
  - Said Muse Spark is "not at the tier of the leading frontier models" (e.g. Claude, ChatGPT) and framed it as an "appetizer," with Meta "cooking" stronger systems now.
  - Bet that consumer health capabilities will be the key differentiator for Meta's future models: "Health is an area that we view as really critical as we scale these models out to billions."
  - Noted Muse Spark outperformed internal expectations, with health among its strongest areas.

## Core theses right now

- Meta rebuilt its AI stack from scratch in nine months; Muse Spark is proof the reset worked, but it's only an "appetizer" — frontier-tier successors are in training.
- The destination is "personal superintelligence": agents scaled to billions of users that know your goals and act for you, not just answer questions.
- Differentiation will come from domain depth — consumer health above all — rather than leaderboard scores.
- Meta has moved to closed frontier models under MSL, a strategic break from the Llama open-weight era.
- He is candid about Meta's current gap with OpenAI/Anthropic/Google while arguing Meta's distribution (billions of users) is the decisive advantage.
