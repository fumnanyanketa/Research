# Yann LeCun

> Turing Award winner and former Chief AI Scientist at Meta (departed late 2025), now co-founder of AMI Labs, betting that world models — not LLMs — are the path to human-level AI.

Window: 2026-04-11 → 2026-06-11 · Compiled: 2026-06-11

## Interviews & podcast appearances

### 2026-05-04 — Axios interview: "AI godfather Yann LeCun's advice on college, work and breaking through AI hype"
- Link: https://www.axios.com/2026/05/04/ai-godfather-survival-guide-hype-doom
- Key ideas:
  - Doom narratives about AI are wrong and "extremely destructive."
  - Listen to economists, not CEOs, on AI job impact — many economists doubt AI will wipe out large swaths of entry-level white-collar work.
  - High school students should still go to college and pursue fields they enjoy; fears about vanishing entry-level jobs shouldn't sway them.
  - Companion Fortune piece (2026-05-05): CEOs hyping AI job loss are "extremely destructive" and "your kids are paying the price" — https://fortune.com/2026/05/05/ai-job-apocalypse-warnings-destructive-yann-lecun/

### ~May 2026 (date unconfirmed) — Unsupervised Learning podcast (Jacob Effron, Redpoint): "Yann LeCun on What Comes After LLMs"
- Link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ngBraLDqzdI (show page: https://open.spotify.com/show/1ILHVeQ1jbI3pClQiy8oN6)
- Key ideas:
  - LLMs are a dead end on the path to human-level intelligence; the industry's need for a paradigm shift away from LLMs will become "completely obvious" by early 2027.

  - Discussed his departure from Meta and his contrarian thesis behind AMI Labs.
  - AMI Labs is pushing forward with its world-model work (JEPA architecture / "LeWorldModel"), funded by a $1.03B seed round.

## Blog posts, essays & notable statements

### Ongoing through the window (reported 2026-06-06 / 2026-06-10) — AMI Labs' $1.03B bet against LLMs; JEPA as the alternative
- Link: https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317928/20260606/yann-lecun-world-models-bet-ami-labs-stakes-103-billion-against-large-language-models.htm
- Key claims:
  - LLMs are limited by reliance on statistical language patterns and fail to capture real-world understanding; they cannot reach human-level intelligence on their own.
  - JEPA (Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture) models the physical world via abstract representations, inspired by how infants learn from observation and interaction (https://www.geeky-gadgets.com/yann-lecun-jepa-replaces-llms/, 2026-06-10).
  - AMI Labs builds "world models" — AI that learns how physical reality behaves rather than predicting the next token.
  - Background: AMI raised $1.03B seed at a $3.5B pre-money valuation in March 2026 (https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/09/yann-lecuns-ami-labs-raises-1-03-billion-to-build-world-models/) — predates the window but anchors his current public argument.

### Context, just before window (2026-04-01) — Brown University Lemley Lecture
- Link: https://www.brown.edu/news/2026-04-01/yann-lecun-artificial-intelligence-pioneer
- Key claims (noting this is ~10 days before the window opens, included as immediate context):

  - LLMs "are completely helpless when it comes to the physical world."
  - Slide to researchers: "IF YOU ARE INTERESTED IN HUMAN-LEVEL AI, DON'T WORK ON LLMs."
  - Also covered by the Brown Daily Herald: https://www.browndailyherald.com/article/2026/04/ai-pioneer-yann-lecun-discusses-new-frontiers-in-the-field-at-brown-lecture

## Core theses right now

- LLMs are a technological dead end for human-level AI; the field's need for a new paradigm will be undeniable by early 2027.
- World models built on JEPA — learning from video, spatial data, and physical interaction — are the path forward, and AMI Labs ($1.03B seed) is his vehicle for proving it.
- AI doom narratives are wrong and "extremely destructive"; CEOs hyping mass job loss are harming young people, and economists (who are skeptical of the job apocalypse) deserve more trust than CEOs.
- AI will amplify human intelligence rather than replace it anytime soon; young people should still get educated and pursue what they enjoy.
- Robotics is headed for a paradigm shift around 2027, driven by grounded, world-model-based AI rather than language models, which he considers intrinsically unsafe as a foundation.
