# Demis Hassabis

> Co-founder and CEO of Google DeepMind (and CEO of Isomorphic Labs), Nobel laureate driving Google's push toward AGI and AI-for-science.

Window: 2026-04-11 → 2026-06-11 · Compiled: 2026-06-11

## Interviews & podcast appearances

### 2026-05-20 — Semafor interview (hours after his Google I/O 2026 closing keynote)
- Link: https://www.semafor.com/article/05/20/2026/google-exec-demis-hassabis-predicts-were-at-the-foothills-of-the-singularity
- Key ideas:
  - Closed Google I/O with the line "We're at the foothills of the singularity," and told Semafor he debated the decision but wanted to "be authentic about what I'm thinking with AGI."
  - His interpretation of "singularity": "the era that we're in" — not a single overnight event.
  - Said he deliberately chose "a little bit provocative" terms to provoke urgency among governments, economists, and the public to prepare for increasingly powerful AI.
  - On agents: "This year, I really felt … that it's the beginning. Agents are starting to work, becoming useful harnesses … coding is starting to work properly."
  - Noted "soft self-improvement" is already underway — coding agents making engineers much more productive.

### 2026-05-26 — Axios interview (after Google I/O)
- Link: https://www.axios.com/2026/05/26/deepmind-ceo-demis-hassabis
- Key ideas:
  - AGI on track for 2030, "plus or minus a year" — and he now sees 2029 as a possibility; confidence is growing that the industry has found the right technical path.
  - Remaining unsolved gaps: world physics, memory, consistency, and continual learning.
  - "We can see agents really happening now and imagine what they will be in another year, and how useful they'll be."
  - Society has only a few years left to prepare for AGI.

### ~2026-05-20–22 — Fast Company profile/interview at Google I/O 2026

## Core theses right now

- We are "at the foothills of the singularity" — an era, not an event — and AGI is on track for ~2030, possibly 2029; he assigns the remaining hard problems to world physics, memory, consistency, and continual learning.
- 2026 is the year agents started genuinely working, including "soft self-improvement" via coding agents that make engineers far more productive.
- He is intentionally using provocative language to push governments, economists, and the public to prepare — society has only a few years.
- AI's most important payoff is science and medicine: Isomorphic's $2.1B raise backs his ambition to "solve all disease," with AI-designed drugs targeting human trials by end of 2026.
- Confidence comes from a converging technical path: Gemini-class models plus agentic harnesses, with DeepMind's agent-first research heritage now mainstream across Google's product line.
