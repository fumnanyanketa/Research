# Elon Musk

> CEO of xAI (now the AI division of SpaceX), Tesla, and SpaceX; spent much of this window in court against OpenAI and restructuring xAI.

Window: 2026-04-11 → 2026-06-11 · Compiled: 2026-06-11

## Interviews & podcast appearances

Note: Musk made no major standalone podcast appearance inside this window that searches surfaced; his Moonshots with Peter Diamandis marathon (recorded Dec 22, 2025, released Jan 6, 2026) and his Davos 2026 WEF conversation predate it. His in-window public statements came mainly via courtroom testimony, the Tesla Q1 earnings call, and posts/announcements.

### 2026-04-22 — Tesla Q1 2026 earnings call
- Link: https://optimusk.blog/blog/tesla-optimus-production-timeline/
- Key ideas:
  - Said Optimus v3 will be unveiled closer to production start, with low-volume production beginning summer 2026 and high volume in 2027.
  - Believes "Optimus could become the largest product in history" — not just Tesla's biggest product — framing the humanoid robot as Tesla's eventual center of gravity (https://blog.robozaps.com/b/tesla-model-s-optimus-robot-factory-conversion).
  - On ending Model S/X production: "It's time to basically bring the Model S and X programs to an end with an honorable discharge, because we're really moving into a future that is based on autonomy."

### 2026-04-29/30 — Sworn testimony, Musk v. OpenAI trial (Oakland, CA)
- Link: https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/elon-musk-testifies-that-xai-trained-grok-on-openai-models/
- Key ideas:
  - Testified that xAI "partly" trains Grok on the outputs of OpenAI's models via distillation, asserting this is general practice: "all the AI companies" train on competing models' outputs.
  - Heated cross-examination over whether he read a term sheet; said he read only "the very first part ... to get a grasp of high-level details" (https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/29/musk-altman-live-updates-day-3-open-ai-trial.html).

### 2026-05-18 — Reaction to jury verdict in Musk v. OpenAI
- Link: https://www.npr.org/2026/05/18/nx-s1-5822366/musk-altman-openai-jury-verdict-claims-dismissed
- Key ideas:
  - A federal jury unanimously rejected all of Musk's claims (statute of limitations) after less than two hours of deliberation, following 11 days of trial.
  - Musk called the verdict a "terrible precedent," vowed to appeal, and repeated: "Altman & Brockman did in fact enrich themselves by stealing a charity. The only question is WHEN they did it!" (https://www.foxbusiness.com/technology/musk-altman-openai-lawsuit-trial-verdict)
  - Time framed the loss as underscoring xAI's competitive struggles (https://time.com/article/2026/05/19/elon-musk-openai-trial-xai-jury-verdict/).

## Blog posts, essays & notable statements

### ~May 2026 — xAI absorbed into SpaceX; Grok team restructuring
- Link: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XAI_(company)
- Key claims:
  - In May 2026, Musk announced xAI would cease to exist as a separate company, with Grok and X becoming the AI division of SpaceX (the SpaceX acquisition itself closed in February 2026); the same month xAI laid off another 10 employees and restructured its Grok team.
  - On June 9, Bloomberg reported xAI tapped a Starlink staffer to run the Grok training team (https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-09/musk-s-xai-taps-starlink-staffer-to-run-grok-training-team).

### ~May 2026 (date unconfirmed) — Colossus 1 lease to Anthropic; pivot toward compute services
- Link: https://puck.news/is-elon-giving-up-on-xai/
- Key claims:
  - Musk revealed SpaceX/xAI cut a deal to lease Anthropic the entire capacity of its Colossus 1 data center (reported at up to ~$4M-scale annual revenue per Puck's account).
  - The deal raised questions about whether xAI is stepping back from the frontier-model arms race to focus on "neocloud" compute services — including Musk's stated ambition to put GPUs in space ("My estimate is that within 2 to 3 years, the lowest cost way to generate AI compute will be in space," per https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/02/elon-musk-spacex-acquires-xai-data-centers-space-merger/).

## Core theses right now

- AGI is imminent on his clock (he has said it arrives in 2026, with AI exceeding combined human intelligence by 2030 — a claim he made pre-window and has not walked back).
- Humanoid robots are the real prize: Optimus "could become the largest product in history," and Tesla is sacrificing legacy EV lines (Model S/X) to build it; autonomy is the company's future.
- OpenAI remains his chief antagonist: despite losing the jury verdict on all claims, he insists Altman and Brockman "stole a charity" and is appealing.
- Distillation of competitors' model outputs is, in his telling, standard industry practice — including at xAI.
- xAI's identity is shifting from standalone frontier lab to SpaceX's AI/compute division, with space-based data centers as the long-term cost play — even leasing Colossus capacity to rival Anthropic.
