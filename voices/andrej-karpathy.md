# Andrej Karpathy

> OpenAI co-founder and former Tesla AI director who joined Anthropic on 2026-05-19 to lead a new team using Claude to accelerate pretraining research.

Window: 2026-04-11 → 2026-06-11 · Compiled: 2026-06-11

## Interviews & podcast appearances

### 2026-04-30 — Sequoia AI Ascent 2026: Fireside chat with Stephanie Zhan
- Link: https://www.theaiopportunities.com/p/sequoia-ai-ascent-2026-andrej-karpathy (see also https://analyticsdrift.com/andrej-karpathy-agentic-engineering-software-3/ and https://x.com/karpathy/status/2049903821095354523)
- Key ideas:
  - Drew a line between "vibe coding" (delegating code to AI without interrogating output) and "agentic engineering" (rigorously directing multiple AI agents on high-judgment tasks): "Vibe coding raises the floor. Agentic engineering is about extrapolating the ceiling."
  - Pinpointed December 2025 as a personal inflection point: in November he wrote ~80% of his own code; by December the ratio inverted, and coding agents began producing large, correct chunks needing no correction ("I can't remember the last time I corrected it").
  - LLMs are about more than speeding up existing workflows — ask "what information transformation was impossible before but is now natural?"
  - Verifiability thesis: traditional software automates what you can specify; LLMs + RL automate what you can verify — which is why math, code, tests, and benchmarks improve fastest.
  - Frontier models are "jagged entities": able to refactor 100k-line codebases or find zero-days, yet unable to reason about trivial physical-world tasks, because RL reward signals concentrate in verifiable domains.
  - Hiring should test agentic engineering directly (build a project with agents, deploy and secure it, then have adversarial agents attack it); understanding becomes the bottleneck — you must know what to ask, inspect, and reject.

## Core theses right now

- The frontier-LLM era's next few years are "especially formative," and being inside a frontier lab is the only way to keep real technical intuition — hence the move to Anthropic and back to hands-on R&D.
- AI is now automating AI development itself: his new team's mandate is using Claude to accelerate pretraining research.
- Vibe coding was the warm-up; "agentic engineering" — directing fleets of fallible agents while preserving correctness, security, and taste — is the new professional skill that raises the ceiling.
- Capability follows verifiability: domains with automatic reward signals (math, code, tests) improve fastest, producing "jagged" models that are superhuman and incompetent at once.
- LLMs create genuinely new capabilities, not just speedups of existing workflows; human understanding (knowing what to ask, inspect, reject) is the new bottleneck.
