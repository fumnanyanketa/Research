4. **Workstream owners** named — at least provisionally. Every workstream needs one name.
5. Agreement on the **one hard deadline: program locked by 31 Dec 2026.**
6. A **next checkpoint date** and a shared place for notes/recordings.

If you only get 1–4, it was still a good meeting. 5–6 can be confirmed in follow-up.

## 3. Run-of-show (≈90–120 min, flexible)

### Block 0 — Settle & frame (10 min)
- Welcome, "we're recording for notes."
- One line on why we're here: *"We've booked the Museum of Technology for Africa Week, 18–25 May 2027. We're starting a full year out on purpose — that's our advantage. Today is about getting the right people aligned and hearing everyone's ideas, not locking everything down."*
- Quick round: name + what they're excited about / want to work on.

### Block 1 — What being part of this means (10–15 min)
- Set the expectation Richmond flagged: this needs **devotion**, not one-week-party energy. Building something over 3–12 months.
- Briefly define **what volunteering/owning a workstream means** here: a workstream has one owner, regular check-ins, and accountability — "you carry your part."
- This filters energy early and is kinder than discovering it in March.

### Block 2 — Ideas workshop (30–40 min) ← the heart of the meeting
Run it open. Two prompts on the wall/board:
1. **"Africa Week 2027 — what should it include?"**
2. **"Generally — what do you want to see/do more of?"** (not just Africa Week)

Facilitation:
- Everyone throws ideas; **no judging yet** — capture all of them.
- You listen for ideas that **align with the tech theme** or with people's stated passions, and note who's energized by what (that's how you find owners).
- Park the big recurring asks: Anthropic relationship, AI literacy workshops, the app, food festival, youth day, the election/Community 2027 angle. Let the room rediscover them rather than you dictating.

### Block 3 — Cluster & assign (20–30 min)
- Group the ideas into the **workstreams** from the master plan (Program, Partnerships, Food & Tourism, Tech & Digital, Marketing, Operations, Finance/Grants, Youth, Cinema).
- For each, ask **"who wants to own this?"** Provisional is fine. Write the name down.
- Flag anything with **no owner** as a gap to fill before the next checkpoint.

### Block 4 — Close with commitments (10 min)
- State the **one hard rule**: program locked by **31 Dec 2026**; Jan–May 2027 is marketing/execution only.
- Name the **immediate next actions** (e.g., Anthropic email, grant applications, website build over the summer).
- Set the **next meeting/checkpoint date** and where notes live.
- Thank people. End on energy, not exhaustion.

## 6. Watch-outs (Richmond's notes, made concrete)

- **Don't over-formalize the open.** Start human; structure comes in Blocks 3–4.
- **Don't let it drift into a party / vibes-only session.** The Block 1 expectation-set prevents this.
- **Keep it small.** Better 6 devoted people than 20 spectators.
- **Don't dominate.** You have 50,000 ideas — hold most of them; capture theirs first. You can thread yours in during clustering.
- **Watch for "present but not there" people** (the engagement problem from the 11 June notes) — note who actually leans in.
