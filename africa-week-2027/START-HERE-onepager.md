# Africa Week 2027 — Start Here

*Welcome. This one page tells you what Africa Week 2027 is, where we are, and how to jump in. Read it once and you're oriented.*

---

## What it is

**Africa Week 2027** is an 8-day celebration of African culture, business, technology, and innovation, "the innovative side of Africa." We are aiming for a world-class experience, the kind of event partners ask to be part of. **The 2027 theme has not been decided yet.** It is one of the first things we will choose together.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dates** | 18–25 May 2027 (8 days) |
| **Venue** | Museum of Technology, Helsinki (~5 halls + outdoor area) |
| **Theme** | To be decided |
| **Audience goal** | 2,000–5,000 people, including international visitors |
| **Why now** | We're planning a **full year out** — that head start is our biggest advantage |

---

## The week at a glance (draft)

- **Opening + exhibition** (runs all week)
- **Technology & Business Day** — talks, startups, investors, partner showcases
- **Lifestyle & Fashion**
- **Youth & Technology Day** — kids' and adults' tracks
- **African Food Festival + Tourism** (2 days) — the big crowd-puller
- **Partner day** + **African cinema**
- **African Day** (final day) — invite-only: embassies, VIPs

*The program is a draft until it's locked on **31 December 2026**. From January 2027 it's all marketing and execution.*
