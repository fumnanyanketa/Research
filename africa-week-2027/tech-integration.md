# Africa Week 2027 — Technology Integration Plan

How tech becomes the fabric of the event, not a corner booth. Ordered by leverage.

---

## 1. The Africa Week app (AI-native event companion)

The single most visible tech statement: an event whose own app demonstrates the theme.

**Core (must-have, lock scope early):**
- Full program: events, times, places, hall maps
- Registration / ticketing integration
- Push notifications ("opening in 30 min in Hall 2")
- Speaker & vendor directory

**AI layer (the differentiator):**
- Conversational assistant ("what's happening right now for kids?", "where do I get jollof?") — grounded only on event data
- Personal schedule builder from interests
- Live multilingual help (English/Finnish/French…)

**Build notes:**
- Build **Android first, iOS later** (Google Play ~€25 one-time vs Apple €99/yr — decision already leaned in the meeting). A responsive web app may cover iOS users for v1.
- v1 scope by January 2027 (registration + program); AI assistant can ship as an update in spring once content is final.
- This is also the Anthropic showcase: "Claude as the event's base technology."

## 3. Exhibition: AI-guided art storytelling

Recreate the "scan the artwork, get the story" experience (the defunct startup a friend ran):
- Visitor scans a QR/image next to each artwork → AI narrates the backstory, history, and context in their language, conversational follow-ups allowed.
- Makes art accessible to people who "don't understand art" — explicitly the goal stated in the meeting.
- Technically simple in 2026: image/QR → grounded chat per artwork. Could be a feature of the event app rather than a separate product.
- Bonus: same mechanism works for food festival dishes (story of each dish/country) and exhibition of African inventions.
