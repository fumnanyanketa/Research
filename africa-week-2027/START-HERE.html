An eight day celebration of African culture, business, technology, and innovation. Read this one page and you will know what we are building, where we are, and how to join in.

The essentials

Dates
18 to 25 May 2027

Theme

We are planning a full year ahead. That head start is our greatest advantage, and it is what will let us deliver a world class experience, the kind of event partners ask to be part of.

The week at a glance

  • Opening and exhibition. Runs all week.
  • Technology and Business Day. Talks, startups, investors, partner showcases.
  • Lifestyle and Fashion.
  • Youth and Technology Day. Tracks for kids and for adults.
  • African Food Festival and Tourism. Two days. The big crowd puller.
  • Partner day and African cinema.
  • African Day. The final day. Invite only: embassies and special guests.

This program is a draft until it is locked on 31 December 2026. From January 2027, the focus is marketing and execution.
