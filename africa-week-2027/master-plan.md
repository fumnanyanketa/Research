# Africa Week 2027 — Master Plan

**Dates:** 18–25 May 2027 (8 days)
**Venue:** Museum of Technology, Helsinki (booked) — ~5 halls + outdoor entrance area
**Theme:** To be decided (technology proposed) · "the innovative side of Africa"
**Attendance target:** 2,000–5,000 across the week, including international visitors
**North star:** Slush-level production quality. Become the event partners ask to join.

---

## 1. What "Slush-level" actually means (and how we get there)

Slush's quality comes from a few replicable disciplines, not budget alone:

| Slush discipline | Our version |
|---|---|
| Program locked & announced months out | Program locked **31 Dec 2026**, marketing-only from January |
| One unmistakable visual identity | Single brand kit (logo, colors, templates) before any partner outreach; everything — site, app, banners, slides — uses it |
| Curated, invite-only core + open fringe | African Day invite-only; the rest open with **registration** (registration = data = professionalism) |
| Partners integrated, not bolted on | Partner packages with defined deliverables, responsibilities (incl. cleaning/setup), and deadlines |
| Volunteer army with clear roles | Recruit volunteers from the AI literacy workshops + open call; every volunteer has a written role card |
| Measured everything | Registration numbers, session attendance, NPS-style feedback via the event app |
| Production values | Stage/AV plan per hall, professional photo/video with written frameworks (post-mortem lessons applied), daily content output during the week |

The moat is the 12-month head start. Protect it: every month of slippage converts directly into last-minute compromises (the embassy-venue lesson).

## 5. Funding strategy

1. **Grants**: city/municipal event & integration funding; digital-skills program funding (for the workshop series); foundations funding bold ideas. Application calendar with deadlines — start now, not months out.
2. **Corporate sponsorship**: tiered packages (headline / day sponsor / hall sponsor / in-kind). Tech companies for the tech day; Anthropic ask is credits + workshop + possible sponsorship (see tech-integration.md). Camera/AV, airlines, food brands for the festival.
3. **Partner fees**: organizations running events during the week contribute a fee or services (the "we take responsibility, you pay your share" principle already used successfully).
4. **Vendors**: food festival stall fees.
5. **Earned**: possible paid masterclasses/workshops.

Risk rule: the budget must work at the **minimum** realistic sponsorship level; sponsorship upside buys staff (the full-time hire) and production polish, not survival.

## 6. Risk register (from hard experience)

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Partner promises venue/critical service and ghosts | Nothing critical rests on an uncontracted promise; written commitments + named backup for every critical item |
| Vendor cancels last-minute (happened with food, 24h before) | Reserve vendor list; deposits; contingency budget line |
| Team burnout (8 events/year already) | Buy out small tasks (cleaning etc.); dedicated coordinators for cinema, youth day; volunteers with role cards |
| Freelancer under-delivery (video post-mortem) | Written framework + references + milestones for every creative engagement; pay on delivery |
| Competing event same dates | Announce early (esp. food festival); claim the calendar publicly in 2026 |
| AI/tech plans outdated by May 2027 (field moves fast) | Keep tech commitments thematic now, lock specific tools/demos only in early 2027 |
| Key-person dependency | Every workstream documented well enough for handover; recordings + transcripts of all planning meetings (already the habit) |

## 7. Success metrics

- ≥ 2,000 unique attendees (stretch 5,000); measurable via registration
- Program locked 31 Dec 2026 (binary)
- ≥ X confirmed sponsors / € raised (set X at kickoff once budget drafted)
- International attendance: visitors from ≥ 3 countries via the international Africa Week network
- App adoption: ≥ 50% of registered attendees use the event app
- Post-event: attendee satisfaction collected in-app; sponsor renewal intent
- Media: press coverage in Finnish mainstream media (the positioning goal made concrete)
