## 4. Operating principles the leadership set (useful guardrails)

- **"Be responsible for your own idea."** Ideas without an owner who'll drive them go nowhere; even a small ownership stake is enough to start.
- **Start with one project, prove the model, then expand** — don't spin up many clubs at once.
- **Reframe sensitive things** so they're accessible (call it "girls club" not "mental health"; build trust through fun first).
- **Trust is two-way and earned over repeated showing-up** — they've been burned by flaky people; the new team is a fresh start.
- **Don't manufacture expectations** (clear expectations or none — avoids frustration on both sides).
- **Be a destination, not a pursuer** — structure + a sign-up form so people ask "how do I join," rather than chasing people.
- **Respect time** — punctuality, and a realistic monthly (not weekly) ask.
