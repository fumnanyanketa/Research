# CLAUDE.md

Guidance for Claude when working in this repository.

## Writing style (applies to every document, file, commit message, and reply)

- Never use em dashes. Do not use the em dash character anywhere, in documents, reports, business plans, filled templates, commit messages, or chat replies.
- Write in natural, flowing sentences. Use ordinary punctuation (commas, full stops, colons, semicolons, and parentheses) where it genuinely helps readability.
- When you would reach for a dash to join or separate clauses, rewrite the sentence, or use a comma, a full stop, or parentheses instead.
- For ranges, use words rather than a dash. For example write "2012 to 2017" and "200 to 300 euros".

## Filling official templates

- When asked to fill a provided template (Word, Excel, PowerPoint), fill only the answer areas that are meant for answers. Do not change the template's own text, headings, structure, or instructions.
- Do not add notes, comments, or bracketed instructions inside a template. Where an answer is not yet known, write "To be decided".
